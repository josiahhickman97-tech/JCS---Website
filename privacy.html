Privacy policy

What the jcssafetysystems.com website collects, and how it is used.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Contact form

When you send the form on our contact page, the details you enter (facility type, name, email, phone if you provide it, service interest, and message) are emailed to our inbox at sales@jcssafetysystems.com through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form delivery service. If the form cannot be sent, your browser opens your email app with the message filled in so you can send it yourself.

We use these details to respond to your request.

Phone and email

If you call (601) 337-6852 or email sales@jcssafetysystems.com, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, and message, and use it to respond.

Google Ads measurement

Every page on this site loads the Google tag (gtag.js) for our Google Ads account (AW-18397273274). It helps us measure visits that come from our ads, and it records a conversion when a contact form message is sent successfully. Google may set or read cookies or similar technologies in your browser for this purpose.

Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Hosting and site analytics

This site is hosted by Vercel. To deliver pages, Vercel processes standard technical request information, such as your IP address and browser type. The site also loads Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page visits so we can see how the site is used.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so your browser connects to Google to download them.

Questions

Questions about this page or a message you sent us? Email sales@jcssafetysystems.com or call (601) 337-6852.
