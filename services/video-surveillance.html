Frequently asked questions

What types of security cameras do you offer?
We tailor recommendations to your site: indoor and outdoor, wired (preferred for reliability) and wireless or cellular when wiring isn’t practical, high-resolution models, LPR, smart AI analytics, and customizable motion detection — from basic monitoring to high-performance multi-site systems.
Do your camera systems include smart AI features?
Yes. Many systems detect and differentiate people, vehicles, animals, and packages to reduce false alerts. For higher precision we offer enhanced detection and license plate recognition (LPR). We’ll match models to your environment during consultation.
Do you offer wireless camera solutions?
We prioritize wired systems for reliability. When wiring isn’t practical, we deploy proven wireless and cellular options so you still get full coverage without compromise.
Is there an app to view cameras remotely?
Yes. User-friendly apps let you view from anywhere, with enterprise-grade control over who can access which cameras and from which devices — ideal for multi-site commercial and industrial use.
Do you offer technical support?
Yes. Our team provides ongoing technical support so your system stays reliable. Phone assistance is available within 24 hours, with on-site service within 48 hours when needed.
