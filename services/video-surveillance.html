Real-world challenges

Problems camera systems usually create

If your current cameras feel useless, it is usually design - not a missing brand name.

Challenge 01

Blind spots where risk lives

Cameras point at empty floor while the back door, POS, or lot approach stays dark. Installers filled a count, not a risk map.

What we design: On-site placement plan for entrances, high-value zones, lots, and sightlines that match how the facility actually runs.

Challenge 02

Hours of video, zero search

When something happens, staff scrub timelines hoping to find a 10-second moment. By then the window for action is gone.

What we design: AI event search, clear retention design, and exports supervisors can use without a specialist on call.

Challenge 03

Motion alerts that cry wolf

Trees, headlights, and shadows train everyone to ignore the phone. Real after-hours activity gets lost in the noise.

What we design: People and vehicle detection tuned for the environment so alerts earn attention.

Challenge 04

Vehicles without identity

Lot and gate cameras show a car shape - not a plate you can match to an incident window or investigation.

What we design: LPR cameras where lighting, angle, and lane geometry support plate capture - common for law enforcement, campuses, and controlled lots.

FAQ

Video surveillance FAQs

What types of cameras do you install?

Indoor and outdoor cameras selected for the environment - wired when reliability matters most, with wireless or cellular options when wiring is not practical. We also deploy LPR where plate capture is a real requirement.

Do systems include AI analytics?

Yes. Many systems detect people and vehicles to reduce false alerts. Enhanced analytics and LPR are matched to the site during design, not bolted on as a default upsell.

Can I view cameras from my phone?

Yes. Secure apps and browser access let managers view live and recorded video, with permission control over who can access which cameras.

Cloud or local recording - which is better?

It depends on bandwidth, IT policy, multi-site needs, and retention. We recommend the architecture that fits the facility - sometimes hybrid.

What about support after install?

Phone help within 24 hours and on-site within 48 hours for covered issues, plus training so your team is not stuck waiting on us for every clip.
