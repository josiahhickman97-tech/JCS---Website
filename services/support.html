Real-world challenges

What bad service looks like on a security system

If support is weak, even good hardware becomes shelfware - or a liability.

Challenge 01

Drop-and-disappear installers

The system goes live, the crew leaves, and nobody knows passwords, camera maps, or who to call.

What we design: Documented handoff, labeled systems, and a clear support path with JCS.

Challenge 02

Nobody trained the actual users

Office staff, facilities, and supervisors never learn search, credentials, or arming - so they stop using the system.

What we design: Hands-on training for the roles that live in the tools every week.

Challenge 03

Friday failures with Monday support

A recorder or door controller dies and the next available appointment is days away.

What we design: Phone help within 24 hours and on-site within 48 hours for covered issues.

Challenge 04

No plan for growth or warranty

Adds and replacements become emergency quotes with no system history.

What we design: Service relationships, warranties, and as-built knowledge that make expansion orderly.

FAQ

Installation and support FAQs

What are your support response times?

Phone assistance within 24 hours and on-site service within 48 hours for covered issues - critical for systems that protect people, inventory, and operations.

Do you service systems you did not install?

Often we can help after an assessment. Some third-party systems are serviceable; others need partial replacement. We will tell you honestly after we see it.

Is training included?

Yes for new installs. We train the roles that will use live view, credentials, arming, and exports day to day.

Can you work nights or weekends?

When the site requires it - retail after close, schools outside instructional time, industrial around shifts - we plan those windows into the project.

Do you offer ongoing service agreements?

Yes. Ask about maintenance and support options so issues are handled under a known plan instead of emergency scramble.
