Access control & keyless entry

Know who entered, when, and through which door — with schedules, remote unlock, and no more re-keying after every staffing change.

JCS Safety Systems installs user-friendly access control that works for a single office door or an entire complex. Choose cloud-based management for remote control or on-premise systems when you need everything local. Keyless and touchless options eliminate lost keys and unauthorized copies.

Key benefits

  • No more key headaches

    Keyless entry and automatic time-stamped logs mean only the right people get in — and you always have proof of who entered and when. No re-keying when someone leaves.

  • Smoother shifts & schedules

    Doors lock and unlock on your schedule. Add or remove access in seconds from a phone — no waiting for the person with the master key.

  • Simple anywhere management

    Mobile credentials or cards, remote lock/unlock, and interfaces your team learns quickly — manage from the office, home, or the road.

Features that keep facilities accountable

  • Time-stamped entry and exit logs for audit trails
  • Scheduled lock settings for office hours, deliveries, and cleaning crews
  • Scalable from one door to multi-building campuses
  • Cloud or on-premise architecture to match your policies
  • Integration evaluation for existing locks and hardware where feasible

Frequently asked questions

Can I manage doors remotely?
Yes. Lock and unlock doors from your phone, tablet, or computer. Add or remove users and monitor activity in real time from anywhere.
Can doors unlock on a schedule?
Yes. Program automatic lock/unlock for office hours, deliveries, cleaning crews, or any routine access need.
Does the system log who entered and when?
Yes. Every entry and exit is recorded in a detailed audit trail you can review anytime.
Do you offer keyless or mobile phone entry?
Yes. We support keyless entry including mobile credentials via smartphone apps — secure access without traditional keys.
Can existing equipment be integrated?
Sometimes. During consultation we evaluate current locks and controllers and recommend integration or a full upgrade, whichever fits best.
