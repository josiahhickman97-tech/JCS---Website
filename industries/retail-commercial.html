Real-world challenges

What retail operators actually fight

Modeled after how leading brands frame retail risk - loss, access, operations - with answers tailored to your floor plan.

Challenge 01

High-value displays get hit first

Shoplifters target open fixtures, endcaps, and zones staff cannot watch every second. Low-resolution or poorly aimed cameras make recovery and prosecution harder.

What we design: Priority camera placement on high-shrink aisles, entrances, and exits with footage managers can search by time and event.

Challenge 02

The back door is a profit leak

Receiving doors and employee exits get propped for smoke breaks, deliveries, or convenience - and become the preferred path for internal and external theft.

What we design: Door contacts, access schedules, and cameras aimed at rear exits with alerts when doors stay open past policy.

Challenge 03

After-hours inventory disappears

When the store is dark, break-ins and opportunistic theft happen fast. Alarms alone do not show what walked out or which vehicle pulled up.

What we design: Perimeter and lot coverage with human and vehicle detection so you get actionable notifications, not noise.

Challenge 04

Cash rooms and offices stay on keys

Keys get copied, shared, and lost. You cannot prove who entered the office, safe room, or stock cage after a shortage.

What we design: Keyless access with time-stamped logs and schedules that match open and close - revoke credentials the day someone leaves.

FAQ

Retail security questions

How many cameras does a retail store need?

It depends on square footage, layout, and shrink hot spots - not a one-size kit. We walk the store, map entrances, POS, stock, and exits, then propose coverage that matches budget and risk.

Can systems help with both external theft and employee theft?

Yes. Floor and exit coverage deters and documents external loss; stock-room, office, and access logs improve accountability inside the operation.

Do you work around store hours?

Yes. Most sales-floor installs are scheduled after close or before open so customers are not navigating ladders during peak traffic.

What if I have multiple locations?

We design for consistency across sites so managers use the same workflows, while still adjusting camera counts for each floor plan.
