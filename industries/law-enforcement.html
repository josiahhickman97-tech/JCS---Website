Real-world challenges

Challenges for stations and secured facilities

When video is evidence, plates matter at the gate, and doors protect sensitive spaces, generic commercial kits are not enough.

Challenge 01

Footage that will not hold up

Low quality, short retention, or unreliable recorders waste investigative time and weaken cases.

What we design: Proper placement, resolution, and recording design so reviews are clear and exportable when needed.

Challenge 02

Vehicles come and go without identity

Parking lots, sally ports, and public approaches see constant vehicle traffic. Standard cameras show a car - not a plate you can search against a BOLO or incident window.

What we design: LPR cameras aimed and lit for plate capture, with searchable plate history tied into your video system where policy allows.

Challenge 03

Evidence and restricted rooms

Shared keys and informal access to evidence, armories, and interview spaces create chain-of-custody risk.

What we design: Access control with detailed logs and limited credentials for high-sensitivity doors.

Challenge 04

Perimeter and lobby pressure

Public lobbies, sally ports, and outdoor perimeters mix public access with secured operations.

What we design: Zoned video, LPR at vehicle control points, and door control that keep public areas open while locking down operational spaces.

Challenge 05

Downtime is unacceptable

A dark recorder or dead door controller is an operational incident - not a ticket for next week.

What we design: Support commitments (phone within 24 hrs, on-site within 48) plus warranties and proactive maintenance options.

FAQ

Public safety FAQs

Do you install LPR cameras for law enforcement facilities?

Yes. We design and install license plate recognition cameras at gates, parking lots, and vehicle approaches so staff can capture and search plates when investigations or facility security require vehicle identity - not just a wide-angle view of a car.

Where does LPR work best on a station or corrections site?

Controlled vehicle points - main entry lanes, sally ports, employee lots, and public parking approaches - where vehicles slow or stop and lighting can support plate capture. We assess angles, speed, and lighting before specifying hardware.

Can you improve an aging station system without full replacement?

Often we can phase upgrades - replace failing recorders or critical cameras first, add LPR at key lanes, then expand coverage and access control as budget allows.

Do you support evidence-room access control?

Yes. Restricted doors with individual credentials and time-stamped logs are a common request for stations and corrections environments.

What are your support response times?

Phone support within 24 hours and on-site service within 48 hours for covered issues - critical when systems support public safety operations.

Will staff be trained on export, review, and plate search?

Yes. A system is only useful if supervisors can find footage and LPR events when needed. Training is part of go-live.
